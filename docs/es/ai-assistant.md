# AI Assistant

The inline contextual AI chatbot for assisting broker workflow.

## Purpose
A chat interface integrated directly within the platform context.

## Usage
- Request draft replies to emails.
- Analyze risks on specific clauses or ship conditions.
- Produce rough freight/TCE estimates (if applicable).

## Credits & Limitations
- Utilizing the Assistant consumes minimal usage credits. Handles 402 effectively.
- **Safe Limitations:** Does not possess authority over the final commercial or legal decisions. No guarantees provided. Always review output drafts.
