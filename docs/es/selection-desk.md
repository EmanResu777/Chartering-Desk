# Selection Desk

The built-in selection tool for analyzing prospective cargoes/vessels.

## Matching & Analysis (`matchVessels`)
- Contextual AI analyzes geometric and commercial feasibility between chosen cargoes and vessels.
- Evaluates risks, routing feasibility, and fit gaps.

## Using Credits
- Deep-dive match analysis consumes usage credits.
- Failed analytical requests gracefully abort without deducting credits.

## Interpreting Output & Limitations
- Output structures highlight pros, cons, and missing parameters.
- AI output is advisory. It reflects an initial commercial review.
- It does not substitute exact voyage estimators.
