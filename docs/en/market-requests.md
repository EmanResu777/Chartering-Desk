# Market Requests

Market Requests allow users to proactively broadcast or silently search for required shipments (Cargo Search) or available ships (Tonnage Search) without relying solely on passive email parsing.

## Adding a Market Request
- **Cargo Search Requests:** Define parameters for cargo you need to move.
- **Tonnage Search Requests:** Define a ship position requiring employment.
- **Available Vessel / Open Tonnage Listings:** Broadcast open positions to the network.

## Matching Engine Structure
The platform continuously compares Market Requests against network data (other requests, shared cargo, open tonnage).
- **Matching Score (0–100):** Calculates spatial and commercial overlap.
- **Match Labels:** 
  - *Strong match*
  - *Possible match*
  - *Weak match*

## Dealing with Matches
- Actions available on matches: **Interested**, **Contact**, **Dismiss**, **Process Offer**.
- Activating a match can trigger notifications to the counterpart or convert the interaction into a Deal Room.

## Privacy & Visibility Settings
- **Private:** Visible only to you.
- **My Desk:** Visible to members of your organization/desk.
- **Desk Network:** Broadcasted securely/anonymously to the wider network.
- **Privacy-Safe Previews:** The system obscures exact vessel names or sensitive terms unless the counterpart accepts the connection or visibility settings explicitly permit it.

## Important Limitations
- Market Requests facilitate negotiation; they do not automatically execute final fixtures.
- Broker-side Recap Confirmation within the app is separate from offline, legally-binding confirmations.
