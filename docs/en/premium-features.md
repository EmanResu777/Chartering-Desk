# Premium Features Roadmap

The Premium Roadmap introduces advanced workflows and intelligent capabilities to Chartering Desk, designed for teams and organizations handling high-volume chartering operations.

## Completed Premium Modules

1. **Smart Deal Radar / Watchlist**
   Continuously monitors incoming data against your watchlists and saved searches (see [Smart Radar & Watchlist](smart-radar-watchlist.md)).
2. **AI Deal Brief**
   Generates a structured, highly analytical breakdown of deals, exposing risk patterns, missing data, and commercial compatibility (see [AI Deal Brief](ai-deal-brief.md)).
3. **Counterparty CRM**
   A dedicated CRM interface to manage contacts, track interactions, and associate private or desk-scoped notes with specific charterers, owners, and brokers (see [Counterparty CRM](counterparty-crm.md)).
4. **Deal Room / Negotiation Room**
   A private collaborative workspace to organize negotiations, store shared documents/notes, manage counterparty visibility, and stage recap drafts (see [Deal Room](deal-room.md)).
5. **Smart Alerts & Daily Digest**
   Provides an intelligent, priority-filtered alerts center and daily summary of relevant deal updates, radar matches, and pending actions (see [Smart Alerts & Daily Digest](smart-alerts-daily-digest.md)).

## Premium Gating

These features operate under the platform's standard usage tiers:
- Access to certain modules requires an active **Desk** or **Enterprise** plan (or trial).
- AI generation tasks (such as AI Deal Brief) consume Usage Credits.
- Admin/Founder accounts bypass normal paywall/credit gating during evaluation.
