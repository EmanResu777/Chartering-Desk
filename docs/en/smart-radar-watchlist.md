# Smart Deal Radar & Watchlist

The Smart Deal Radar is a premium surveillance engine that continuously monitors all incoming platform activity against your specific parameters.

## Watchlists
You can configure persistent watchlists (e.g., specific trading routes, cargo types, deadweight ranges) to flag any relevant activity automatically.

## Radar Sources
The Radar Engine scans:
- Your own Cargo and Vessel assets.
- Assets published to the Desk Network.
- Active Market Requests.
- Available Vessel / Open Tonnage listings.
- Hot Opportunities.
- Inbound AI-parsed emails safely indexed in real-time.

## Intelligent Scoring
- Results are ranked with a score of `0-100`.
- Matches are categorized automatically: `Excellent`, `Strong`, `Possible`, `Weak`.
- The Radar provides the *reasons* for the score and *suggested actions*.

## Actions
- **Save Match:** Pin the match to your dashboard.
- **Dismiss Match:** Remove the match from your feed.
- **Process Offer / Deal Room:** Convert an excellent match immediately into an active Deal Room.

## Data Boundaries
- **Premium Gating:** Access depends on billing/subscription tier.
- **Legal Perimeter:** No final fixture is generated automatically through radar matches.
