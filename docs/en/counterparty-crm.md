# Counterparty CRM

The Counterparty CRM module tracks external relationships, historical interactions, and internal notes associated with the market operators.

## Access & Profile Management
- Accessed via the primary navigation or the dedicated Profile Drawer slide-out.
- The Counterparty Linker allows associating CRM entities with active deals.

## Supported Counterparty Types
- Broker
- Charterer
- Owner
- Operator
- Trader
- Manager
- Agent
- Other

## Managed Fields & Data points
- **Identity:** Name, Company, Country/Region.
- **Contact:** Email, Phone, Messenger.
- **Vessel/Cargo preferences:** Trading Area, Preferred Cargo, Preferred Tonnage.
- **Tags & Indicators:** Status (Active, Watching, Blocked), Custom Tags, Reliability Indicator, Response Status.

## Note Tracking & Privacy
- **Private Notes:** Users can log interactions tracking specific event types:
  - Call
  - Email
  - WhatsApp
  - Meeting
  - Offer
  - Recap
  - General
- Notes can be strictly private to the author, or shared with the user's Desk (`my_desk`).
- Entities such as Cargo, Vessel, Market Requests, Offers, and Deal Rooms are automatically linked in the CRM profile when interactions occur.

## Anti-Abuse Controls
- **No Public Ratings:** Counterparty reliability and status indicators are entirely private/internal.
- **No Global Scoring:** There is no AI reputation scoring or shared global rating board.
